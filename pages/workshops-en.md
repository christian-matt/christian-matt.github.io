Formats

## Three formats, one standard

From an impulse for a large audience to a confidential session with the board.

- *30 to 60 minutes*

  ### Keynote

  A sharp talk that makes a topic tangible, provides orientation and invites further thought. For corporate events, conventions and conferences.

- *60 to 120 minutes*

  ### Board session

  A compact, confidential briefing for executive and supervisory boards as a basis for upcoming decisions.


- *Half or full day*

  ### Interactive workshop

  Working together on your concrete questions: current position, use cases, guardrails. With results you take away.
