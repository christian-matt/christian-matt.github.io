Christian Matt investigates how digital technologies – especially AI – reshape organizations, industries, and society at large. His research is driven by a central question: How can we harness the potential of digital transformation and AI while ensuring responsible, human-centered design?

To answer this, he takes an interdisciplinary approach – combining insights from information systems, management and other disciplines – and works closely with partners from sectors such as e-commerce, healthcare and public services. His studies explore the real-world impact of AI-based services and digital strategies on key stakeholders, from customers, patients and professionals to regulators and executives.

His work is published in leading academic and practice-oriented journals, including <i>MIS Quarterly</i>, <i>Journal of Management Information Systems</i>, <i>Journal of Information Technology</i>, and <i>MIS Quarterly Executive</i>. He also serves as Senior Editor for <i>Electronic Markets</i> and <i>Internet Research</i>. Stanford University's 2025 global ranking recognizes him among the world's <i>Top 2%</i> most impactful scientists.
