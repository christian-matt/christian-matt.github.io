Executive education

# Building capability that lasts

Education for executives and specialists that anchors digital and AI capability in your organisation for the long term. From open programmes to tailored in-house modules, always with the aim of making knowledge applicable.

Offerings

## Three ways to build capability

- *University of Bern*

  ### Open programmes & modules

  Modules within executive education programmes (including CAS) at the University of Bern, where leaders engage with digital transformation and AI on a rigorous, practical footing.

- *In-house*

  ### Tailored in-house modules

  Programmes shaped around your sector, your roles and your concrete challenges. For teams that want to build capability together.

- *Contribution*

  ### Contribution to your programme

  A substantive guest lecture or impulse as a component of an existing executive or leadership programme.

---

## What distinguishes the programmes

- **Research-based**  
  Current findings rather than buzzwords, prepared for teaching.
- **Applied**  
  Exercises and examples that connect to your day-to-day work.
- **At a high level**  
  Content that takes experienced specialists and executives seriously.
