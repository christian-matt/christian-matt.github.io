Bookable formats

## How we can work together

Three entry points, depending on the question and the depth required. Each format can be tailored to your context.

- *Tailored*

  ### AI potential and readiness assessment

  A structured outside view of your starting position: where the most valuable use cases lie, what is realistic technically and organisationally, and which steps are worth taking first.

  **Outcome:** prioritised use cases and a robust roadmap.

- *Half, or full day, or ongoing*
  
  ### Executive briefing & strategy sparring

  A focused exchange with your leadership team: putting developments in context, testing assumptions, sharpening options. Available as ongoing support.

  **Outcome:** clarified priorities and a shared factual basis.

- *Project-based*

  ### Transformation guidance

  Support from design through to implementation: as a sparring partner for internal teams, in steering committees, or at critical turning points.

  **Outcome:** decisions that are carried, and an initiative that gains traction.
